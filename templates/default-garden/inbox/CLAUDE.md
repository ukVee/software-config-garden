# inbox/

Triage drawer. Drop a note here when you don't yet know where it belongs, then file it into the right concept dir later.

## How to behave here

- A quick capture beats a misfiled one. Use a short descriptive filename.
- When triaging, move the note to its real home and leave nothing stale here.
- If something genuinely has no category, that's a signal a new concept dir may be needed — raise it with the user rather than inventing one silently.
